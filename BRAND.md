# EADA Brand System

## Brand idea

**Surplus into value.** EADA makes circular-economy decisions feel clear, credible, and commercially useful. The identity combines circular motion with a forward arrow: resources return to the economy while value moves ahead.

## Logo

- `assets/logo-mark.svg`: primary symbol for icons, avatars, favicons, and compact surfaces.
- `assets/logo-lockup.svg`: bilingual horizontal signature for headers, documents, and presentations.
- Keep clear space equal to one quarter of the mark width on every side.
- Minimum digital size: 24 px for the mark and 140 px for the lockup.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolor individual paths, add effects, or place over visually busy imagery.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Deep Green | `#0B3B32` | Trust, navigation, primary dark fields |
| EADA Green | `#0F745A` | Core actions, highlights, progress |
| Growth Green | `#16A078` | Status and positive signals |
| Value Gold | `#D6B65C` | Premium accents and value moments |
| Circular Sand | `#F4F0E6` | Warm sustainable backgrounds |
| Canvas | `#F8F7F2` | Main application background |
| Ink | `#0B2621` | Primary text |
| Slate | `#68736F` | Supporting text |

Use green as the dominant brand color, sand as the environmental neutral, and gold sparingly to signal recovered value.

## Typography

- Arabic: IBM Plex Sans Arabic, weights 400–700.
- Latin and numbers: Inter, weights 400–800.
- Headlines are compact and confident; body copy uses generous line height.
- Use Inter numerals for scores, values, and metrics.

## Voice

Clear, practical, and optimistic. Explain the commercial decision first, then the sustainability benefit. Avoid exaggerated AI claims, vague environmental language, and promises not supported by verified data.

Preferred terms: “indicative value,” “decision signal,” “best-fit route,” and “recoverable value.” Always label illustrative marketplace data and prototype estimates.

## Interface principles

1. **Decision first:** show the recommended route and confidence before supporting detail.
2. **Evidence visible:** attach context and caveats to every estimate.
3. **Value in motion:** use arrows, progress, and directional composition to express circulation.
4. **Bilingual by design:** RTL and LTR layouts carry equal hierarchy and polish.
5. **Quiet confidence:** warm whitespace, crisp cards, restrained gold, and purposeful motion.
